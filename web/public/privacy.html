Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Profyn helps D2C brands see what each order contributes after refunds, returns, shipping, fees and ads. This page explains what we store, where, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Account: when you sign in with Google, GitHub or email, we store your name, email address, profile picture link, sign-in provider and the time of each sign-in. We never receive your Google or GitHub password.
  • Your data: the order files you upload, the analyses computed from them, and the scenarios and decisions you save.
  • Preferences: your language and whether AI wording is switched on.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1), India. Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Uploaded files are kept in a private storage bucket that is not publicly readable.

Who can see it

Data you upload while signed in is tied to your account, and other accounts cannot read it. The public demo uses synthetic sample data that is not linked to anyone. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI

Every rupee figure in Profyn is computed by our own calculation engine, not by an AI model. Optional AI wording (Google Gemini) only explains those figures. It receives summary numbers such as totals and counts by cause, never your raw order rows. AI wording is off for your uploaded data unless you turn it on, and Profyn works fully without it. Your data is not used by us to train any model.

Your choices

  • You can use the demo without an account.
  • You can ask us to export or delete everything linked to your account at any time; signed-in users also get Export and Delete buttons in their profile.
  • You can switch AI wording off at any time.

Cookies

We use one sign-in cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser's local storage for your language choice. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
